Our approach

Start with the site. Agree the work. Plan how the planting will be cared for.

Understand your site

Share your location, photographs and what you want to change. Light, water, access and existing planting help define the brief.

Define the work

Discuss plant choices, materials, layout and installation needs. Agree the scope and responsibilities before the work begins.

Plan for ongoing care

Confirm watering, pruning, visits and any replacement arrangements so the landscape or office planting can be cared for after installation.

Landscaping questions

Quick answers about our services and how to get started.

What landscaping services does ANZ provide?

ANZ provides terrace gardens, vertical gardens, villa landscaping, pergola and fabrication, commercial landscaping and garden maintenance. Office plant rental and other planting services are also available.

Who does ANZ Landscaping work with?

ANZ works on residential, commercial and corporate spaces, including corporate offices, hotels, real estate developments and educational institutions.

Where is ANZ Landscaping based?

ANZ Landscaping is based in Indiranagar, Bengaluru, Karnataka. Contact the team with your site location to discuss service availability and project requirements.

Does ANZ provide ongoing garden maintenance?

Yes. ANZ provides regular garden care, pruning and upkeep. Contact the team to discuss the tasks and maintenance schedule your landscape needs.
