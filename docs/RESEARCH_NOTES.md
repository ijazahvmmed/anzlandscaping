# Research and evidence

The supplied WXR was parsed in full: 531 items, including 38 published pages, 410 attachment records, 39 Elementor library records, 34 menu items and four posts. The Forminator JSON was read separately. The PRD and execution prompt informed the build after the user explicitly requested implementation.

The local page map retains the 15 service paths, nine project paths, separate landscape landing page, core/legal and utility records. Two new pillar pages are added only to the isolated local seed; production setup creates drafts explicitly.

Public ANZ homepage content was fetched and corroborated against the export. The Gamlaa reference was opened visually, and its public service-led narrative was reviewed. Belgrade Arbor and GetLayers were fetched for research; their exact runtime easing or hidden implementation was not copied or claimed verified. The Taste Skill was inspected; its screenshot/critique workflow was used, with the direct user’s later demand for shapes, annotations, blur and stronger motion taking precedence over its house-style exclusions. Its full scripted draw/inspector run was not completed, and is not represented as passed.

Office Plant Rental’s source Yoast description refers to FRP planters. A narrowly scoped Yoast description filter corrects only that page; no saved Yoast records are overwritten. Existing legal policies contain generic retail wording and an unrelated privacy link; local seed extraction fixes the broken privacy destination but the policy text still requires approval. The theme does not represent it as new reviewed legal advice.

Missing inputs: full production backup/staging clone, Elementor Pro binaries/licence and active plugin state, SMTP/inbox access, current analytics/advertising tag inventory, Search Console/top landing pages, asset/publicity permissions, current contact confirmation and approved legal copy. No production writes or live submissions occurred.

On 30 October the client supplied [Reslink](https://www.reslink.org/) as the new design-language reference. Its homepage and subsequent visual sections were inspected in the browser. The current ANZ layout adapts its light canvas, compact navigation, centered composition and modular visual containment, without copying its imagery, claims or source code. The user also explicitly authorized realistic generative edits based on existing ANZ photos; five curated derivatives are recorded separately from unchanged originals.
